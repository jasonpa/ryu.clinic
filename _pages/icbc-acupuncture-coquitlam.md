        <dt>Does ICBC cover acupuncture after a car accident in BC?</dt>
        <dd>Yes. Under Enhanced Care, ICBC pre-approves 12 acupuncture treatments in the first 12 weeks after a BC crash, whoever was at fault. No referral needed. Bring your claim number and PHN, and we bill ICBC directly.</dd>

      <div class="faq-pair">
        <dt>How many sessions does ICBC cover, and what happens after 12 weeks?</dt>
        <dd>12, including your first visit, within 12 weeks (84 days) of your crash date. After that, your acupuncturist can send your ICBC recovery specialist a treatment plan asking for more. ICBC decides.</dd>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-pair">
        <dt>Do I need to pay anything at Ryu Clinic?</dt>
        <dd>No. We charge no user fee for ICBC patients and bill ICBC directly. Please give at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule. ICBC doesn't pay for missed or late-cancelled visits.</dd>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-pair">
        <dt>Does it matter who was at fault?</dt>
        <dd>No. ICBC covers you regardless of fault, and pedestrians and cyclists usually get the same benefits.</dd>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-pair">
        <dt>Can I switch to Ryu Clinic from another clinic during my ICBC claim?</dt>
